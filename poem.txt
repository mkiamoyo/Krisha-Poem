Some Things Are Meant to End and Heal and Grow
------------------------------

Not every truth needs to be said,
Not every thought needs to be spread.
For some will hear what they believe,
While others listen and perceive.

Some lessons come through pain and tears,
Through silent nights and passing years.
Though broken hearts may take time to heal,
Some wounds reveal what words can't reveal.

Not every person is meant to stay,
Some come and leave along the way.
And though it hurts to say goodbye,
We learn to heal and grow with time.

So let the past remain behind,
Keep every lesson in your mind.
For life goes on, and so must we,
Sometimes letting go sets us free.
